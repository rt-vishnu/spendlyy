Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly to us. This includes your email address when you create an account, and any expense data you choose to log, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

We do not collect sensitive financial information like credit card numbers or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to deliver and improve the Spendly service. Specifically, we use your expense data to generate summaries, charts, and insights about your spending patterns.

Your data is used solely for providing the service and is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

While we take every reasonable precaution to secure your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or other functionality. These third parties may have access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to maintain confidentiality.

We do not share your personal expense data with third parties for their own marketing or commercial purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal data at any time. You can export or delete your expense data through your account settings.

If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the application.
